# Ad Copy: Oral Probiotic (ProDentim, ClickBank)

Traffic: **Facebook + Native (Taboola / Outbrain)**, one ad set per angle, $50 split equally (~$16.67 each), each pointing at its own page.
Meta headlines are 40 characters max, descriptions 30 max, native headlines 60 max (checked).

**Before launch:** health and supplement ads get extra review on both networks. On Meta, never assert or imply a personal attribute or health condition about the viewer ("Your bad breath...", "Do you have gum problems?"); the copy below describes situations or the category instead. Target 18+. On Taboola / Outbrain, keep claims to the product category, never to disease, and expect a manual review. Bridge pages and ads never name the brand.

Affiliate link: the ClickBank hoplink, tagged per page with `tid=pain`, `tid=curiosity`, `tid=benefit` so ClickBank reports split by angle.
